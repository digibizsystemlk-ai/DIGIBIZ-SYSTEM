Madawala Tea Shop — User Guide

1. Give Credit / New Loan

Click on "1. Give Credit / New Loan" card on the Home Screen. Select the customer from the dropdown, choose the category, type the credit amount, and click "Confirm & Issue Credit".

2. Collect Debt / Repayments

Click on "2. Collect Debt / Repayments" card on the Home Screen. Select the customer to automatically see their total balance due. Enter the paid amount, select payment method (Cash, Bank, or Cheque), and click "Confirm & Record Repayment".

3. Banking & Cash Movement

Click on "3. Banking & Cash Movement" card. Choose whether depositing cash to bank or withdrawing money for shop operations.
